    Ownership registry

    Checking.

    Around 60 MB from the FAA, a couple of minutes on a decent connection. Rebuild every week or so to pick up new registrations.

    FAA registration data is public record, and for individually owned aircraft the registrant address is often a home address. Strip Bay keeps street detail off the strips and shows it only on a registration card.
